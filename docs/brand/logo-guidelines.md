# Logo guidelines

Use `components/brand/Logo.tsx`; do not inline SVG copies. Primary, white and dark lockups,
wordmarks and symbols live in `apps/web/public/brand/`.

- Minimum full logo width: 120 px digital.
- Minimum symbol size: 16 px; prefer 24 px or larger.
- Safe area: at least one quarter of the symbol width around every side.
- Use primary/dark on light surfaces and white on navy or photographic dark surfaces.
- Use the symbol alone for favicons, app icons and constrained square placements.
- Do not recolor individual paths, stretch, rotate, outline, add effects or place over busy imagery.

The mark combines a distinctive B, home/shield silhouette and teal roof/check accent.
