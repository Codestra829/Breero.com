# Odoo pipelines

Customer stages: New Intake, Qualification, Needs Customer Information, Customer Contacted, Provider Coordination, Appointment Follow-Up, Estimate Follow-Up, Customer Decision, Post-Service Follow-Up, Converted / Booking Created, Closed — Cancelled, Closed — Unserviceable, Closed — Lost.

Provider stages: New Interest, Initial Review, Information Requested, Documents Requested, Qualification Review, Service-Area Review, Interview / Call, Approved for Onboarding, Onboarding in Progress, Onboarded, Rejected, Withdrawn. Approval never activates a BREERO provider.

Support cases use New, Assigned, Customer Contacted, Waiting on Customer, Waiting on Provider/Internal Team, Under Review, Resolved, Closed. Disputes retain their backend status/deadline and use CRM review activities; Odoo cannot issue a credit, refund, replacement lead, or final decision.
